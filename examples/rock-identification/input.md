# Rock app idea

I'm a hobby rockhound and I want an app for people like me.

You take a photo of a rock or mineral with your phone and the app tells you what it is.
It should use a powerful AI model in the cloud so the results are accurate.
It also needs to show how sure it is about the answer.

People should be able to save the rocks they find into their own collection,
with the photo, the name and where they found it.
Users can save identified rocks to their personal collection.

A lot of us go collecting in the mountains with no signal, so the app has to work
completely offline in the field.

It must be fast and easy to use, even for kids.

Later maybe an admin who can fix wrong rock descriptions in the database.

I don't want to pay for servers if possible.
